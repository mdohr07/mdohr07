## About me

After several years as a freelance artist and designer, I retrained as an **IT Specialist in Application Development
** because I wanted to expand my skill set toward a powerful combo. That design background still shapes how I work: I think visually — in layout, hierarchy, and usability — as much as in components and state.

I'm currently looking for a junior frontend or fullstack role, ideally somewhere that values clean UI, accessible code, and people who ask questions. Outside of code, I draw in ink and build fictional worlds for tabletop RPGs.
